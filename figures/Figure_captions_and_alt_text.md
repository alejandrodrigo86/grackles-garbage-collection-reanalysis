# Grackle manuscript figures

## Figure 1. Foraging activity across clock time and collection phase

**Caption.** (A) Model-adjusted Great-tailed Grackle foraging activity across local clock time. Curves show expected foraging seconds per 30-min interval for non-collection days and for collection days before and after collection ended; translucent bands are 95% confidence intervals. The three curves are predictions from one shared clock-time basis, not separately fitted phase trajectories. On the log-link scale, scenario coefficients shift the shared curve additively (and therefore multiplicatively on the response scale). Short ticks along the top mark the 12 observed collection-end times. Different horizontal extents reflect restriction to the clock-time ranges supported by each phase. (B) Descriptive, unadjusted foraging rates before and after collection on each of the 12 collection days. Each line joins the two phase-specific rates from one observation day. Observation 2 had only 3.3 min of pre-collection exposure and is identified explicitly. Panel B displays heterogeneity and should not be interpreted as a time-adjusted treatment effect.

**Alternative text.** Two-panel figure. Panel A shows three smooth daily curves. The collection-day post-collection curve is generally above the collection-day pre-collection curve, but their confidence bands overlap widely. The non-collection curve lies between them for much of the day. Collection ended between approximately 10:00 and 13:45. Panel B connects before- and after-collection raw rates for 12 days. Eleven lines rise and one falls; increases vary greatly. Observation 2 rises from zero after only 3.3 min of pre-collection exposure.

## Figure 2. Event-centred change after garbage collection

**Caption.** (A) Adjusted foraging rate ratio as a function of elapsed time since garbage collection ended, relative to the collection-day pre-collection period. The orange line is the event-centred model estimate, the translucent band is its 95% confidence interval, and open points mark saved model contrasts at 0, 0.5, 1, 3, and 6 h. The model-implied ratio at collection end was 1.93 (95% CI 1.20-3.11), followed by a multiplicative change of 0.86 per additional hour (95% CI 0.76-0.97). The central estimate reached 1 after approximately 4.3 h. (B) Camera exposure contributing to successive one-hour post-collection windows; labels give the number of observation days represented. Elapsed post-collection time was strongly correlated with clock time, so this figure describes a temporal association and does not directly demonstrate food depletion.

**Alternative text.** The adjusted rate ratio starts near 1.93 at collection end and declines steadily on a logarithmic scale, crossing the no-change line at about 4.3 h and reaching about 0.77 at 6 h. The confidence band widens over time and includes 1 after roughly the first two hours. The exposure panel shows about 12 camera-hours and all 12 collection days in each of the first four hourly windows, falling to about 9.5 h from 10 days in hour 4-5 and about 7.8 h from 9 days in hour 5-6.

## Figure 3. Adjusted effect-size summary

**Caption.** Adjusted ratios and 95% confidence intervals for the designated primary outcome and for secondary, sensitivity, and exploratory estimates involving the mechanism decomposition, supporting foraging responses, recorded group size, competitor-adjusted displacement activity, and elapsed post-collection time. All rows except the final one compare collection-day post-collection periods with collection-day pre-collection periods after adjustment; the final row is the multiplicative change for each additional post-collection hour. The vertical dotted line at 1 denotes no change. Colour and shape identify evidentiary groups and do not encode statistical significance. Only total foraging duration was designated as the primary contrast; the remaining estimates are hypothesis-generating and were not multiplicity-adjusted.

**Alternative text.** Forest plot with nine estimates on a logarithmic ratio scale. The primary overall-foraging estimate is 1.30 with a confidence interval from 0.84 to 2.02. Exploratory recorded focal visibility is 1.35, whereas conditional foraging intensity while visible is 1.02. Recorded group size is 1.25 with a confidence interval from 1.04 to 1.49. Competitor-adjusted displacement is 0.66 with a broad interval from 0.22 to 1.98. The exploratory hourly post-collection multiplier is 0.86 with a confidence interval from 0.76 to 0.97.

## Figure 4. Recorded group size across clock time and collection phase

**Caption.** (A) Adjusted time-weighted mean recorded group size across local clock time. Curves represent non-collection days and collection days before and after collection ended; bands are 95% confidence intervals and short ticks mark the 12 collection-end times. (B) Descriptive pre- and post-collection means for the 11 collection days with known group states in both phases. Eight increased and three decreased. Count states covered 98.1% of focal-visible time. The open-ended 9+ category was represented by the lower bound 9. Replacing 9+ with 12 or 15 produced similar estimates, whereas excluding affected intervals weakened the adjusted ratio from 1.25 to 1.14. Group states do not identify independent birds.

**Alternative text.** Two-panel figure. Adjusted curves in Panel A show expected recorded group size near 1.5 to 1.8 before collection and near 2.0 to 2.3 after collection, with overlapping confidence bands. Panel B shows 11 paired collection days; eight lines rise and three fall.

## Figure S1. Leave-one-day-out robustness checks

**Caption.** Leave-one-day-out estimates from models refitted after omitting each of the 25 observation days. (A) Post- versus pre-collection foraging rate ratios with 95% confidence intervals. (B) Hourly post-collection multipliers, shown as point estimates for a direction-of-effect check. Orange triangles indicate omission of a collection day; open blue circles indicate omission of a non-collection day. Dashed orange lines mark the full-data estimates, and dotted black lines mark a ratio of 1. Observation 2, which had only 3.3 min of pre-collection exposure, is flagged. All 25 overall estimates remained at or above 1, and all 25 decay estimates remained below 1.

**Alternative text.** Two aligned leave-one-day-out plots. In panel A, refitted overall rate-ratio estimates range from approximately 1.00 to 1.45, with all confidence intervals crossing 1. Omitting Observation 2 yields an estimate close to the full-data value. In panel B, all hourly multipliers fall between approximately 0.83 and 0.90, remaining below the no-change value of 1 regardless of which day is omitted.

## Accessibility and export notes

- The palette avoids red-green contrasts and is based on colour-vision-accessible blue, orange, green, purple, and neutral grey.
- Colour is always paired with line type, point shape, direct labels, or position.
- Uncertainty intervals are shown wherever they are available; statistical significance is not encoded through colour intensity.
- PNG files are exported at 600 dpi. PDF and SVG files are vector versions suitable for resizing and publication production.
- Captions distinguish adjusted estimates from descriptive raw rates and state the major inferential limitations directly.
